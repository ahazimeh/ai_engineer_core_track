The paper is "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani et al. It introduced the Transformer architecture, which relies entirely on self-attention mechanisms instead of recurrence (RNNs) or convolutions. It became the foundation for virtually all modern large language models (BERT, GPT, etc.).

tricks to make model perform better: (training time scaling and infrance time scaling)
    infrance time scaling techniques:
        -reasoning: we can let the model do better by asking it to generate tokens that describe how you will answer the question, or use the word wait while it is thinking
        -another type of infrance time scaling is about putting more information in the input sequence (such as rag)

early days neural networks were trained at the character level (predicts the next character in this sequence)
    too much work from the network
then neural networks were trained off words (predict the next word in this sequence)
    easier to learn but leads to enourmous vocabs with rare words omitted
the breakthrough was to work with chunks of words, called tokens
    middle ground: manageable vocab, and useful information for the neural network in addition, elegantly handles word stems
    https://platform.openai.com/tokenizer
    rule of them (1 token is ~4 characters, t token is 0.75 words)

import tiktoken
encoding = tiktoken.encoding_for_model("gpt-4.1-mini")
tokens = encoding.encode("Hi my name is Ed and I like banoffee pie")

tokens to text:
for token_id in tokens:
