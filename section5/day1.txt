Auto-regressive LLM
    train to take input sequence and to predict the next token that should comes after
Auto-encoding
    produce output based on the full input

Auto-encoding LLMs
    -applications include sentiment analysis and classification
    -also used to calculate "Vector Embeddings", representing an input as a list of numbers -ie. a vector

The vecotrs mathematically represents the 'meaning' of an input
    -can represent a character, a token, a word, an entire document, or something abstract
    -Typically have hundreds, or thousands of dimentions
    -Represent an 'understanding' of the inputs; similar inputs are close to each other
    -Support 'vector math' like the famous example: "King - Man + Woman = Queen"
